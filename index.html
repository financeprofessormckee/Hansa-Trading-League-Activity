How to build your league
  1. Each city has 100 labor per turn. Choose outsiders to invite, then divide production labor between grain and cloth.
  2. Add shipments between participating cities. The exporter pays transport labor before production. Goods must come from its own output.
  3. Preview consumption and utility. Newcomers must beat autarky (their best outcome without trade). Members taking a loss will leave after the turn.
  4. Execute six turns. Your score includes all gains and losses relative to autarky; there are no carried inventories.

Utility = 2√(grain × cloth), a balanced-consumption index, not money. Equal labor shares maximize autarky utility. All costs and outcomes are deterministic; repeated plans repeat their results.
